About Me

I'm M O C H R M, an independent artist blending music with activism. My songs challenge societal norms, address political tension, and awaken critical thinking. Inspired by underground movements, I use music as a protest and expression of identity, from street struggles to digital revolution. Born in Indonesia, raised by culture, shaped by justice.
